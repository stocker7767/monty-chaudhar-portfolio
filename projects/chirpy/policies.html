About this demo.

Important information

This is an independent speculative redesign for presentation purposes. It is not Chirpy's official website, is not endorsed by Chirpy, and does not accept real orders, payments or bookings.

Products & pricing

All catalogue products and prices are fictional placeholders. Any real purchase must be completed through the official Chirpy website.

Privacy

The demo shopping bag is stored locally in your browser via localStorage. The contact form opens an email draft and does not transmit information to this website. No account system, analytics or marketing signup is implemented. External images and Google Fonts may contact their respective providers when loaded.

Images and brand

Photos are illustrative and not claimed as photos of Chirpy or its inventory. Business contact information is included to demonstrate a potential website layout.

Production requirements

Before a real launch, the owner would need to approve content and branding, provide real catalogue data and photos, and connect payments, stock, workshop booking, delivery, customer support and legally reviewed policies.
