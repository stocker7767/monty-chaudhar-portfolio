1. Who is responsible

ABHIKA AURA LTD, company number 17186771, is responsible for personal information it receives and uses to manage enquiries and its own client relationships. Our displayed location is Leeds, LS9 8FE.

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact insights@abhikaaura.com or +44 7442 302822. This notice concerns our website and ordinary enquiry and client administration. Work involving a client’s systems or personal data is addressed in the relevant project and data-processing terms.

2. Information and sources

We receive the information you choose to send by email or tell us by telephone, including your name, business or organisation, email address, telephone number, project requirements and correspondence. If you become a client, relevant records may include contractual, invoicing, payment-reference and project-administration information.

Information normally comes directly from you. A person contacting us for a business may provide another relevant business contact’s details where authorised. We do not use this enquiry form to collect payment card details or passwords.

Hosting and security services may process technical connection information such as IP address, browser information, requested page, access time and security events to deliver and protect the website. Sign-in information for the private hosting platform is managed by that platform under its own privacy arrangements; this website does not provide an Abhika Aura account system.

3. How the enquiry form works

Information typed into the current form is used locally in your browser to prepare an email. Clicking “Prepare enquiry email” opens your email application; it does not send the message or submit its contents to a website database. Review the draft and press Send in your email application to contact us.

The “Copy enquiry” button copies the prepared text to your device clipboard only when you ask it to. Your email application, browser and device may retain drafts or autofill information according to their settings. Our application code does not save form contents in cookies, local storage or session storage.

Once you send an email, we and the email providers involved handle that correspondence. Closing the form is not a deletion request for a message already sent.

4. Purposes and lawful bases

Purpose Lawful basis

We only use information necessary for the relevant purpose. We do not use this website to carry out automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects, advertising profiling or behavioural analytics.

5. Who may receive information

Access is limited to people who need information for the relevant enquiry, engagement or legal purpose. Relevant recipients may include authorised staff, contractors bound by suitable obligations, email and hosting providers, IT service providers, and professional accounting or legal advisers. Authorities may receive information where disclosure is legally required.

We do not sell personal information. We do not send initial website enquiry contents to an AI model through this website. If a project proposes external AI processing or additional suppliers, that processing must be considered and disclosed in the project arrangements before use.

Providers processing information for us must have appropriate safeguards and contractual terms where required. Hosting, sign-in or communication providers may also process some information independently under their own privacy notices. Contact us for the relevant provider information for your enquiry.

6. International processing

Email, hosting or technology providers may process information in countries outside the UK. Where a restricted international transfer is made on our behalf, we use a permitted mechanism, such as applicable UK adequacy arrangements or appropriate contractual safeguards, together with any required assessment.

We do not promise that all data remains in the UK. You may ask us which providers and transfer safeguards apply to information we hold about you and request details or a copy of applicable safeguards, subject to lawful restrictions.

7. Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose, applicable legal requirements and the resolution of complaints or claims.

  • Enquiries that do not become a project: our standard retention approach is up to 12 months after the last substantive contact, unless an ongoing request, complaint or lawful need justifies longer retention.
  • Contract and financial records: our standard approach is up to six years after the engagement ends, or for a different period where applicable tax, accounting or legal requirements justify it. This does not mean keeping every technical file or every piece of personal data for six years.
  • Technical and security logs: retention depends on the provider’s operational and security arrangements. We do not specify an unverified universal retention period for hosting-platform records.

Records subject to a genuine dispute or legal hold may need to be kept longer. Unnecessary information is deleted or anonymised, subject to controlled backup cycles and lawful retention. Contact us for the retention criteria applying to your records.

8. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law gives you rights to access your personal information, correct inaccuracies, request erasure, restrict processing, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and receive qualifying information in a portable format.

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting processing already lawfully carried out. You may object to direct marketing at any time. Some rights have exceptions, including where records must be kept by law or for legal claims.

We respond to rights requests without undue delay and normally within one month, subject to lawful identity checks, clarification, extensions or exemptions. We may ask for enough information to verify your identity. Requests are normally free; any lawful fee or refusal is explained.

9. Security and sensitive information

We use appropriate organisational and technical measures to protect information, taking account of its nature and the risks. No communication or system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Do not put medical details, incident reports identifying people, passwords or other sensitive information into the general enquiry form. A project involving health, safety or other special-category information requires a suitable lawful basis, secure handling and the appropriate contractual arrangements before access.

This website is intended for people seeking professional services and is not aimed at children. Contact us if you believe information about a child has been supplied inadvertently.

10. Cookies, external services and changes

Read our Cookie Policy for the current application configuration and the distinction between our pages and hosting-platform technologies. This website code contains no analytics or advertising tags.

When you follow an external link or use an email or telephone application, that service’s own privacy information applies. We review this notice when our processing changes. The date above identifies the current version; material changes are communicated where required.

11. Privacy complaints

To make a data protection complaint, email insights@abhikaaura.com with the subject “Data protection complaint” and explain your concern. You may also call us if you need help making a complaint. We acknowledge receipt within 30 days, investigate appropriately and communicate the outcome without undue delay.

You can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator. Visit ico.org.uk for its current contact and complaint options. Your right to seek an appropriate legal remedy is unaffected.
