2. What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by a browser. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, tracking pixels and techniques that access information on a device. Their purpose and behaviour determine the applicable rules; a technology is not exempt simply because it is not called a cookie.

4. Hosting, sign-in and security

The private hosted version may require the hosting platform’s sign-in. That platform may use cookies or similar technologies for authentication, access control, service delivery or security. These are not set by the Abhika Aura page JavaScript, and this application does not provide controls over another platform’s sign-in cookies.

Check the hosting or sign-in provider’s current notices and your browser’s site-data information for the names, providers, purposes and lifetimes of technologies it actually uses. We do not give invented cookie names or durations. You can ask us for information about the hosting environment.

Technical server logs, such as records of page requests and security events, are distinct from cookies but may contain personal information; see our Privacy Policy.

5. Consent and your choices

For storage or access technologies that require consent, we must obtain your informed, freely given choice before use. Continuing to browse, accepting general terms or submitting an enquiry is not consent to optional tracking. Relevant legal exceptions apply only where their conditions are met.

Our current application has no optional tracking categories to enable, so it does not show an “accept all” cookie banner or save a cookie-consent preference. This is not a claim that a hosting or sign-in platform uses no cookies.

You may inspect, block or delete website data through your browser settings. Blocking authentication or security technologies may prevent access to a private hosted version. Your email application’s storage is controlled through that application.

6. Future changes and contact

If analytics, advertising or other optional integrations are added, we will update the inventory, purposes and retention information and implement any required consent or objection controls before activating them. Where consent is used, refusing must be as easy as accepting, and you must be able to withdraw it.

We review this policy if the website or hosting configuration changes. For questions, contact insights@abhikaaura.com.
