THE GOOD STUFF. NOTHING EXTRA.

Just a few seats, a friendly welcome and a regularly changing selection of well-kept beers. The Black Rat is the kind of place where the best entertainment is the conversation.

You'll find us on Leeds Road, in a former florist's shop that has become a proper neighbourhood favourite.
