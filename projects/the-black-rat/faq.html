Good to know

THE LITTLE DETAILS.

Everything you might want to know before you pop in.

Do you serve food?

The Black Rat is primarily a drinks-focused micropub, rather than a food-led venue. Please call to confirm any current snacks or food arrangements.

Can I reserve a table?

Space is very limited, with capacity commonly described as around 15 people. Please call the pub directly to ask about seating.

What beers do you have?

The cask selection rotates regularly, often featuring Yorkshire breweries. Ask the team what's pouring today.

Are dogs welcome?

Small dogs are generally welcome, subject to available space.

Is there outdoor seating?

There may be seating outside the front of the pub in good weather.

Is the pub accessible?

There is level access from the street, but the interior is compact and access to the single toilet is narrow. Please call ahead if you have specific accessibility requirements.

Is there music or live sport?

The pub is known for conversation rather than music, television or games machines.
