A PROPER LITTLE LOCAL

Small in size, generous in welcome. A Yorkshire micropub built around excellent beer and conversation.

FROM FLOWERS TO FINE ALES.

Set in a former florist's shop on Leeds Road, The Black Rat has become a much-loved part of Thackley's pub scene. Under new ownership since September 2022, the micropub continues to focus on quality drinks and a welcoming atmosphere.

There are no televisions, games machines or music competing for your attention. Here, good conversation takes centre stage.

With room for roughly 15 people, the atmosphere is intimate, informal and unmistakably local.
