What is on the pumps?

GOOD BEER. GOOD PEOPLE.

Our selection changes regularly. These are the styles you can typically expect — not a live drinks menu.

01 / Cask Ale

Up to four changing cask ales, often from Yorkshire breweries. One pump is commonly dedicated to dark beers.

02 / Real Cider

Real cider may be available, including locally sourced options. Availability varies.

03 / Keg Cider & Lager

Craft keg cider and lager for a refreshing alternative.

04 / Artisanal Gin

A choice of gins for those who fancy something different.

Please note: No current beer names, ABVs, prices or stock levels are claimed here. Call or visit to find out what's on today.
