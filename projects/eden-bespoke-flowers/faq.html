Discuss your flowers ↗
Can I place an order on this website?

No. This is an independent redesign concept, not Eden’s official ordering platform. Please telephone the florist on 0113 258 7007.

Can I request specific flowers?

Eden specialises in bespoke arrangements. Call to discuss the flowers, colours and style you prefer. Availability depends on the season and notice.

Do you offer delivery?

Eden advertises delivery throughout Leeds, with local delivery from £5. Ask the shop about available dates, areas and final charges.

Can I discuss wedding flowers?

Yes. Eden invites customers to contact them for a consultation and no-obligation quotation. Availability must be confirmed directly.

Are the photographs actual Eden arrangements?

No. Photographs on this speculative concept are illustrative and should be replaced with licensed, approved images of Eden’s work before any official launch.

Are prices final?

No. Displayed starting prices reflect Eden’s published information and may have changed. Confirm directly before ordering.
