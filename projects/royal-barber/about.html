About the shop

A sharper local experience.

This concept site is designed around what a strong independent barber website should do: explain the offer clearly, show the work, build trust and make booking effortless.

Royal Barber Shirley

Built around the client journey.

The website puts the essentials first: what services are available, how much they cost, where the shop is, what the experience feels like and how to secure an appointment.

Instead of replacing an existing booking system, the site works as the professional front door while Setmore continues to handle appointment slots.

What the finished live version could add

  • Real shop photography and barber profiles.
  • Instagram feed or recent haircut portfolio.
  • Verified Google reviews.
  • Custom domain and professional email.
  • Analytics, Search Console and local SEO.

What matters

Simple. Clear. Bookable.

Clear pricing

Visitors understand the service options before they click through to book.

Mobile first

The layout is designed to work comfortably on phones, where local service searches often happen.

Fast action

Booking buttons stay prominent throughout the site and hand off directly to Setmore.
