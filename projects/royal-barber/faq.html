Frequently asked questions

Before you book.

How do I book?

Use any Book Now button on this website. It opens the existing Royal Barber Shirley Setmore booking page, where you can select a service and available time.

Do I pay on this website?

No. This concept website does not collect payment information. Booking and any payment arrangements remain within the business’s existing booking process.

Can I walk in?

Availability can change, so booking ahead is the safest option. The live Setmore page is the best place to check appointment slots.

Where is the shop?

The public booking profile lists the business at 3 St James Road, Southampton.

Are children’s cuts available?

Yes. The public booking menu includes a kids-under-10 service.

Are the prices on this site final?

This is an unofficial concept site. Always confirm current pricing on the live booking profile before publishing or booking.
